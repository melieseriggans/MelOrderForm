✦ My Story ✦

I'm a self-made 23-year-old who turned a love of baking into something real. ✨

It started with just me, a mixing bowl, and zero shortcuts. That part hasn't changed — I still measure every scoop, roll every ball of dough, and pull each tray out myself. No factory, no middleman, just butter, sugar, and a whole lot of care going into every box.

Order for yourself, your bestie, or someone who needs a little pick-me-up. Whatever the reason, I hope every bite feels like it was made just for you — because it was. ♡
