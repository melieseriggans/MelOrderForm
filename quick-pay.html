Looking For Your Reader

Turn on your BBPOS WisePad 3 (or other Bluetooth reader) and make sure it's near this device.

One-Time Reader Setup

Before your reader can connect, Stripe needs a "Terminal Location" for it. In your Stripe Dashboard, go to Settings → Terminal → Locations, create one (or use an existing one), then paste its Location ID below. It looks like tml_xxxxxxxxxxxx. You'll only need to do this once — this device will remember it.
