🗓️ What Sends On Its Own

So you always know what's about to land in someone's inbox without you touching anything.
On A Schedule
Pickup Reminder — daily, ~10:00 AM Central*
To customers whose pickup/delivery is tomorrow. Same-day orders are skipped — they already got a confirmation.
Weekly Business Digest — Sunday nights, ~9:00 PM Central*
To you only — a recap of the week's orders, revenue, top flavors, and low stock. Doesn't appear in the log below since it never leaves your own inbox.
Site Health Check — every 6 hours
Silent unless something's actually broken — only emails you when a problem is newly found or newly fixed. Also doesn't appear in the log below.

Order Confirmation
To the customer, plus a copy to you, the instant a website order comes through.

Restock Notice
