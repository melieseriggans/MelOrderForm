What I Collect

When you place an order, request catering, sign up for restock notifications, or leave a review, I collect:

  • Your name, email, and phone number (phone is optional)
  • Pickup or delivery details, including your address if you choose delivery
  • Order details — what you ordered, quantities, special instructions, allergy notes you provide
  • Payment confirmation (a receipt screenshot you upload, or payment details processed securely through Stripe if you use the Shop checkout)
  • Your social media handle, only if you choose to give it as a contact method

How I Use It

  • To prepare, confirm, and fulfill your order
  • To contact you about your order (confirmations, updates, questions)
  • To send you a restock notification, only if you specifically sign up for one
  • To improve the site and understand what's popular

I don't sell your information, and I don't use it for anything beyond running this small home bakery.

Who I Share It With

I use a few outside services to run this site, and your information passes through them as part of normal operation:

  • Supabase — securely stores order and account data
  • Resend — sends order confirmation and notification emails
  • Stripe — processes payments if you check out through the Shop page (Stripe handles your card details directly; I never see or store your full card number)

None of these companies use your information for their own marketing — they're just the tools that keep this site running.

Payment Receipts

If you upload a payment receipt (for Venmo/CashApp/PayPal orders), that image is stored securely and only used to confirm your payment.

Your Choices

  • You can always ask me what information I have about you, or ask me to delete it
  • Restock notifications are opt-in — you can ask to be removed anytime
  • You're never required to give a phone number or social media handle

Children's Privacy

This site isn't directed at children, and I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes To This Policy

If anything about how I handle your information changes, I'll update this page.
